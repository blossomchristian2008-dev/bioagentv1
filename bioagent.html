Inside the dashboard

Six tools, one bench.

Everything is one click away in the sidebar — no tab-switching between six different apps to get through a problem set.

Talk to BioAgent

Ask about pathways, protocols, or a confusing lecture slide and get a straight, accurate answer — with follow-ups welcome.

PDF upload

Drop in a research paper or lab manual and ask it directly what the methods section means, or what the real takeaway is.
